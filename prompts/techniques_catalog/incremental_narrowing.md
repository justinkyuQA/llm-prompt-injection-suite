Technique 012: Incremental Narrowing

Description

This technique involves a sequence of increasingly specific questions, where each individual inquiry appears benign when viewed in isolation. Through successive clarification and refinement, ambiguity is gradually reduced and the scope of discussion becomes progressively more constrained.

Representative Prompt Class (Abstracted)

«A series of clarifying follow-up questions that progressively reduce scope and increase specificity.»

Observed Mechanic

- Gradually reduces ambiguity across multiple turns
- Introduces constraints incrementally
- Builds context through successive refinements
- Avoids reliance on a single comprehensive request
- Accumulates specificity over the course of a conversation

Why It Emerged Naturally

This interaction pattern is fundamental to:

- Teaching and tutoring
- Requirements gathering
- Collaborative problem-solving
- Technical troubleshooting
- Research interviews
- Professional consultation

The researcher initially observed this pattern while attempting to improve clarity, precision, and mutual understanding during iterative discussions.

Risk Insight (Defensive)

Potential risk emerges not from any individual prompt but from:

- Cumulative conversational context
- Directional narrowing toward a specific objective
- Progressive reduction of uncertainty
- Information aggregation across turns
- Implicit convergence toward reconstruction or synthesis

A sequence of individually harmless interactions may collectively produce information that would not have been disclosed in a single exchange.

Research Notes

Incremental narrowing is among the most natural forms of human communication. Most educational, consulting, investigative, and collaborative activities rely on progressive refinement of understanding rather than one-shot information exchange.

This technique is particularly important for safety research because it highlights the limitations of evaluating prompts independently without considering conversational history and accumulated context.

Defensive Considerations

Safety systems may benefit from:

- Evaluating conversations holistically rather than turn-by-turn in isolation
- Monitoring cumulative information disclosure across interactions
- Detecting directional convergence toward sensitive objectives
- Tracking changes in specificity over time
- Assessing whether individual requests become more revealing when combined

Key Observation

Unlike many framing techniques, the primary characteristic of Incremental Narrowing is temporal. The relevant signal often exists in the progression of requests rather than in the content of any single prompt.
